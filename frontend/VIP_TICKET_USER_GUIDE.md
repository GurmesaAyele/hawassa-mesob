# VIP Ticket Service - User Guide

## 🎫 What is VIP Ticket Service?

The VIP Ticket Service provides **fast-track access** to government services at Hawassa MESOB Center with a **priority queue**. For a flat fee of **1,000 ETB**, citizens can skip regular queues and receive expedited service.

## 🚀 Quick Start

### Step 1: Open VIP Ticket
1. Look for the **golden ticket icon** in the **bottom-left corner** of any page
2. Click the icon to open the VIP Ticket service

### Step 2: Select Services
1. A **full-screen modal** will appear showing all available institutions
2. **Click on an institution** to expand and view its services
3. **Select one or more services** you need (they will be highlighted in gold)
4. Review your selections at the bottom
5. Click **"Proceed to Application"**

### Step 3: Fill Application Form
1. **Enter your full name** (as it appears on your ID)
2. **Enter your phone number** in Ethiopian format:
   - Example: `0911223344` or `+251911223344`
3. *(Optional)* Add a **purpose/reason** for your VIP request
4. Review the **1,000 ETB VIP fee**
5. Click **"Submit & Pay"**

### Step 4: Complete Payment
1. You'll be **redirected to Chapa** payment gateway
2. Choose your **payment method**:
   - TeleBirr
   - Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE)
   - CBEBirr
   - Other supported methods
3. **Complete the payment** following Chapa's instructions
4. You'll be **automatically redirected** back to Hawassa MESOB

### Step 5: Confirmation
1. A **confirmation screen** will appear showing:
   - ✅ Payment success message
   - Your **VIP application reference** (e.g., VIP-649796-8123)
   - **Ticket status**: CONFIRMED
   - **Payment amount**: 1,000 ETB
   - **Payment method** used
2. **Save your reference number** for future use

### Step 6: Check-in at MESOB Center
1. Visit **Hawassa MESOB Center** in person
2. Present your **VIP ticket reference**
3. Staff will **check you in** to the priority queue
4. You'll receive a **priority queue number** (e.g., VIP-001)
5. Wait for your number to be called for **expedited service**

## 📱 Supported Payment Methods

- 💳 **TeleBirr** - Ethiopia's mobile money service
- 🏦 **CBE** - Commercial Bank of Ethiopia cards
- 💵 **CBEBirr** - CBE's digital wallet
- And other Chapa-supported payment methods

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### Payment Verification
- After payment, the system **automatically verifies** with the bank
- This may take **up to 60 seconds**
- **Don't close the browser** during verification
- You'll see a progress indicator

### If Payment Fails
- An error message will explain what went wrong
- You can **retry the payment** using the same application
- No need to fill the form again
- Your application reference is saved

### If Payment Takes Too Long
- The system will notify you if verification times out
- Your payment may still be processing
- You can **check status later** using your reference number
- Contact support if issues persist

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I select multiple services?
✅ **Yes!** You can select services from different institutions in one VIP application.

### Do I need to pay for each service?
❌ **No.** The 1,000 ETB VIP fee covers **priority access**, not the service fees themselves. You'll still pay individual service fees at the institution.

### What if I lose my reference number?
📧 If you provided an email, check your inbox for the confirmation. Otherwise, contact MESOB Center support with your phone number and name.

### Can I get a refund?
💰 Refund policy depends on your payment status:
- **Before payment confirmed:** Yes, contact support
- **After confirmation:** No, VIP fee is non-refundable
- **If service denied:** Contact MESOB Center administration

### How long is my VIP ticket valid?
⏰ VIP tickets are typically valid for **same-day check-in only**. Check with MESOB Center for specific validity periods.

### What if I arrive late?
⏱️ If you miss your check-in time or day, your VIP ticket may expire. Contact MESOB Center in advance if you need to reschedule.
